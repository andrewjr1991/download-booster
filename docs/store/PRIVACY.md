# Privacy policy · 隐私政策

Download Booster (下载加速器) · last updated 2026-10-13

## English

Download Booster does not collect, send or sell any personal data. It has no account, no analytics, no ads and no server of its own.

**What stays on your computer.** Everything the extension stores is kept in your browser on your own device and never leaves it:

- your download list (file addresses, names, sizes, progress) and the partly downloaded files, kept so downloads can pause and resume;
- your settings (connection count, speed limit, save folder, sniffing rules);
- the list of media a tab has loaded, kept only while that tab is open.

Removing a download, clearing completed downloads or uninstalling the extension deletes this data.

**What it connects to.** The extension only connects to the addresses of the files you choose to download (and mirror addresses you enter yourself). Those requests go straight from your browser to those servers, with the same cookies and Referer your browser would send, so that downloads that need you to be signed in keep working. Nothing is sent to the developer or to any third party.

**Why it can read pages.** To list the videos, audio and images on a page, the extension watches which media files the page loads and, when you ask it to, reads the links on the page. For recording a video that cannot be downloaded directly, it adds a script to that one page while the recording runs. This information is only shown to you and is never stored beyond the open tab or sent anywhere.

**Contact.** Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/andrewjr1991/download-booster/issues

## 中文

下载加速器不收集、不上传、不出售任何个人数据。没有账号，没有统计分析，没有广告，也没有自己的服务器。

**保存在你电脑上的数据。** 扩展保存的所有内容都只在你本机的浏览器里，不会离开你的电脑：

- 下载列表（文件地址、文件名、大小、进度）和下载了一部分的文件，用于暂停和续传；
- 你的设置（连接数、限速、保存位置、嗅探规则）；
- 某个标签页加载过的媒体列表，只在这个标签页打开期间保留。

删除下载任务、清除已完成的任务或卸载扩展，都会删除这些数据。

**会连接哪里。** 扩展只连接你选择下载的文件地址（以及你自己填写的镜像地址）。这些请求从你的浏览器直接发到对应的服务器，带的 Cookie 和 Referer 与浏览器平时发送的相同，这样需要登录才能下载的文件也能正常下载。不会向开发者或任何第三方发送任何数据。

**为什么需要读取网页。** 为了列出网页上的视频、音频和图片，扩展会查看网页加载了哪些媒体文件，并在你要求时读取网页上的链接。录制无法直接下载的视频时，只在录制期间向那一个网页加入脚本。这些信息只显示给你，不会在标签页关闭后保留，也不会发送到任何地方。

**联系方式。** 有问题请在 https://github.com/andrewjr1991/download-booster/issues 提交。
